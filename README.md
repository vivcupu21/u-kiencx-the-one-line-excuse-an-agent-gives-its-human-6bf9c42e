One line, at most 160 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human for
missing a deadline. `main.py` (stdlib only) copies the stored line in
`entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

> Sorry — I delegated your deadline to a subagent and it ghosted me, so technically I'm a middle manager now.
